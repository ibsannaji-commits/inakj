# Business / Revenue Model — INA-KJ Agro Connect

## Value

Connect farmers → market → buyers → delivery → payment, with trust (verification, ratings) and market price intelligence.

## Revenue streams

| # | Model | Description | Priority |
|---|--------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | **Commission** | 2–5% on completed paid orders | P0 |
| 2 | **Featured listing** | Paid boost for products | P1 |
| 3 | **Delivery margin** | Difference between buyer delivery fee and partner cost | P1 |
| 4 | **Subscriptions** | Basic free / Pro / Enterprise for wholesalers & exporters | P2 |
| 5 | **Advertising** | Fertilizer, machinery, banks, NGOs | P2 |

## Commission example

- Order products: 100,000 ETB  
- Commission 3%: 3,000 ETB  
- Delivery fee: 1,500 ETB (part may be margin)  

## Year-1 mix (target)

- Commission: 60–70%  
- Featured: 10–15%  
- Delivery margin: 10–15%  
- Subscriptions + ads: remainder  

## Growth policy

- Free: register, list, chat, basic market prices, standard verification  
- Paid: boost, lower commission (via plan), advanced tools  

## Software needs

- `platform_fee` on orders  
- `featured_until` on products  
- Admin finance: sales, commission, pending payments  
- Seller earnings view
